How ordering works

From DM to pick-up

Please reach out 1 to 2 weeks before your event so there's time to make it just right.

  1. Send a DM

    Message @zocobanners on Instagram with the size you want.

  2. Share your vision

    Tell me the event, your theme, colors, graphics and words. Inspo pics are welcome.

  3. Say when

    Let me know the date you need your banner by.

  4. See your mock-up

    I'll send a mock-up of your design. Payment is due once you've seen it.

  5. Pick it up

    Grab your banner in McAdenville or Gastonia, NC.
